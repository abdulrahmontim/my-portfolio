## What I'm learning
Understanding high-frequency switching power conversion, MOSFET gate driver requirements, dead-time control to prevent shoot-through currents, and PCB layout considerations for minimizing electromagnetic interference (EMI).

## Build plan
1. Simulate the LC filter and PWM switching stage in LTspice.
2. Select high-efficiency N-channel MOSFETs and a dedicated half-bridge driver IC.
3. Breadboard prototype at lower current to verify PWM waveform generation.
4. Design a two-layer PCB with tight loop return paths.
5. Solder components, perform thermal imaging under load, and verify efficiency.

## Parts list
- 1x Dual N-Channel Power MOSFET (30V, 15A rating)
- 1x Synchronous Buck Controller / Driver IC
- 1x 10µH Power Inductor with low DC resistance (DCR)
- 2x 22µF Low-ESR Ceramic Capacitors (Input)
- 2x 47µF Low-ESR Ceramic Capacitors (Output)
- Current sense shunt resistor (10mΩ)

## Results
Initial breadboard tests show stable 5V output under 500mA load with an efficiency of 88%. The switching frequency is running stably at 350kHz.

## What I'd change
For the next revision, I would route the feedback sense line further away from the noisy switching node (SW) and add a thermal via array beneath the lower MOSFET for improved heat dissipation.
