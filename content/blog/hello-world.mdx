Welcome to my portfolio and engineering log.

### Bridging Electronics and Software

Before university, my foundation began in electronics. That background continuously shapes my engineering philosophy: **I care deeply about what happens when things go wrong, not just when they work.**

Whether dealing with ground loops and noise in a power circuit or transient network timeouts and race conditions in distributed backend services, resilient systems require anticipating failure modes from first principles.

### The Learn-and-Build Process

This blog complements my self-set engineering challenges. Each challenge is designed with a concrete definition of done—a verifiable goal—and documented through:
- Technical design notes and simulation tradeoffs
- Real-world measurements and edge cases encountered
- Key lessons learned and iterative improvements
